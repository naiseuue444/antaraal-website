1. Who we are

Antaraal ("Antaraal", "we", "us", "our") operates an aerospace sourcing platform from Unit 101, Oxford Towers, 139/88 HAL Old Airport Road, H.A.L II Stage, Bangalore North, Bangalore 560008, Karnataka, India. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, Antaraal is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. This policy covers this website and the services provided through it.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Applications and requirements — company name, your name, work email, phone, industry or operation type, the products or capabilities you offer or need, certifications, procurement volume and any notes you add.
  • Quote requests and messages — the details of a part or requirement and any contact information you include.
  • Newsletter sign-up — your email address.
  • Correspondence — the content of emails or messages you send us, including support and correction requests.

Information collected automatically

  • Log and device data — IP address, browser type and settings, operating system, referring pages, pages viewed and timestamps. Our hosting and infrastructure providers process this to serve and secure the site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see section 4. We store your cookie choice, and lightweight items such as your recent form entries, in your browser.

Information from other sources

  • Public vendor information — business name, address, contact details, website, capabilities and certifications compiled from public directories, company websites and similar sources, so buyers can discover suppliers. This may include the name and work contact details of a business representative where those were published.

3. How we use information

  • to operate the platform, show vendor listings and let buyers and vendors find and contact each other;
  • to pass a requirement or quote request to the vendors who can respond to it, at your request;
  • to respond to your messages and support requests, including listing corrections;
  • to send the newsletter if you signed up, and to send service or transactional messages;
  • to understand how the site is used and to improve it, using analytics only where you have consented;
  • to keep the platform secure, prevent abuse, and comply with law, including export control and sanctions screening where relevant;
  • to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising by third parties.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of essential cookies and browser storage to run the site — for example to remember your cookie choice and to hold form state while you are on a page. These do not need consent.

With your consent, we use analytics to measure traffic and improve the site. You choose this through the cookie banner shown on your first visit, and you can change your choice at any time from the cookie preferences link in the footer. Declining analytics does not affect your access to the site.

5. Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract or steps at your request — to provide the platform and process your submissions;
  • Consent — for analytics cookies and for the newsletter; you can withdraw consent at any time;
  • Legitimate interests — to run, secure and improve the platform, to compile a useful supplier directory from public information, and to understand and grow our business, balanced against your rights;
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws.

6. When we share information

  • With the other side of a sourcing conversation — if you send a requirement, quote request or application, we share it with the relevant vendors or buyers so they can respond.
  • Service providers — hosting, storage, email delivery, analytics and security providers who process data on our instructions under appropriate contracts.
  • Legal and safety — where we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is needed to enforce our terms, protect the rights, safety or property of Antaraal or others, or comply with export control and sanctions requirements.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

7. Vendor information from public sources

If you are a business representative and a listing contains your information, you can ask us to correct or remove it, or to stop displaying your contact details, by emailing support@antaraalspace.com. We will action reasonable requests promptly. Vendors can also claim a listing to keep it accurate.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above: submissions and correspondence for as long as your relationship with the platform is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping and legal purposes; newsletter data until you unsubscribe; log data for a short period for security and diagnostics. Public directory information is kept while the listing is useful and updated or removed on request. Data held only in your browser stays until you clear it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including transport encryption (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send classified information, controlled technical data, or sensitive personal data through the site.

10. International transfers

We are based in India and our providers may process data in India and other countries. Where personal data is transferred across borders, we take steps to ensure a comparable level of protection, such as using providers with recognised safeguards and appropriate contractual terms.

11. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where it applies, the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data, or restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, where the law provides for this;
  • complain to a data protection authority — in India, the Data Protection Board.

To exercise a right, email support@antaraalspace.com. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within the time required by law.

12. Children

The Service is for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "last updated" date above and, for material changes, take reasonable steps to notify users. Please check this page periodically.
